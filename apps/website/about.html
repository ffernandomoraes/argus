About Argus.

An infinite canvas to keep track of all your projects and Claude Code chats at once.

The name

A hundred eyes on your agents.

In Greek mythology, Argus was the hundred-eyed giant who saw everything at once. The app does the same for your agents: each project folder becomes a block on the canvas, and inside it you see what every chat is doing right now and which ones are waiting on you.

The problem

Working with AI now means working with many agents.

Today almost all work is done with AI, across several projects at the same time. Each chat is stuck in a terminal or an editor tab: there's no overview, what a subagent received and returned stays hidden, and you can't talk to several projects side by side.

Principles

The chat comes first

Typing, pasting screenshots and following the reply have to be fast and pleasant. It's what you do most in the app.

On your subscription

Argus uses the Claude login already on your machine, with no pay-per-use API key.

Everything visible

Nothing an agent does stays hidden: input, tools used and output.

Spatial and personal

The layout is yours: you drag, group and color, and the app remembers.

Connected

Projects, chats, agents and subagents are visually linked.

Start small

Each part has to be useful on its own before moving on to the next.

Who makes it

Made by people who use it every day, open to anyone who wants to help.

Argus is created by Fernando Moraes, Head of Design Engineering, and grows with its contributors. The code is open on GitHub under the MIT license with the Commons Clause: anyone can use, study, modify and contribute to it, but can't sell Argus or services built on it.
