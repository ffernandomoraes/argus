Work and personal, each with its own Claude account

Why Argus ties a Claude account to each canvas group.

ProductJoão AndradeOct 31, 2026

Blog › Product

A lot of people have two Claude accounts: one from work and a personal one. In the terminal, switching between them is a hassle, and it's easy to burn through the wrong account's limit.

The account lives in the group

In Argus, each canvas group uses one account. The Work group uses the company account; Personal uses yours. The group's folders, terminals and chats inherit the account, without you having to think about it.

Accounts that run together

Each account you add lives in its own folder, passed to claude through CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR, with a separate login. That's why the accounts can run at the same time. History, CLAUDE.md, agents and skills stay linked to your main account's: you don't lose anything.

Your limit in view

The indicator in the title bar shows each account's usage limit, without spending usage to check it. Before you start, you can tell which account still has room.
