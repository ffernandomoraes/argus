▦Canvas

All your projects on one canvas.

Each folder becomes a block with its git branch, uncommitted files and latest conversations. Group by work, freelance and personal, and see at a glance what's running.

Download for free Explore Argus

Video of Argus with sample projects and conversations.

More than a project list

Colored groups

Gather folders, terminals and conversations into regions of the canvas, like Figma frames. Collapse or hide the contents whenever you want.

Status for every conversation

Running, waiting on you or done: each row's color and label show instantly what needs your attention.

Git in view

The branch badge shows how many files are uncommitted. One click lists them and opens the diff.

Sticky notes

Leave reminders on the canvas, like Figma comments. Press C or right-click.

Undo for the layout

Dragged something by mistake? Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes, in every canvas window.

Organize by voice

“Create a Freelance group with these two folders.” The command bar understands text and voice.

One account per group

Work and personal, each with its own account.

Each group uses the Claude account you choose. The group's folders, terminals and conversations inherit that account, and each one's usage limit stays visible in the title bar.

  • Minimap with each group's color
  • Extra canvas windows for another monitor
  • The layout comes back exactly as it was when you reopen the app
  • argus . in the terminal opens the current folder

Get started

Up and running in under 5 minutes.

No API key and no server in between. Download, open your folders and go.

  1. 1

    Download Argus

    macOS, Windows or Linux, straight from the GitHub releases.

  2. 2

    Sign in with Claude

    Argus uses the claude on your machine and the subscription you already have.

  3. 3

    Open a folder

    Drag it onto the canvas or run argus . in the terminal.
