?Tutorial · Accounts

Use your company account for work and your own for personal projects

Several Claude accounts, one per group, with each usage limit in plain sight.

Intermediate3 min read

Help › Accounts

  1. 1Add the account

    In Settings › Accounts, click Add account and sign in. Give it a nickname and choose which account is the default.

  2. 2Link the account to a group

    Right-click the group › pick the account. It shows up as a tag next to the group's name.

  3. 3That's it

    Folders, terminals and chats in the group use that account. Anything outside a group uses the default account.

  4. 4Keep an eye on the limit

    The indicator in the title bar shows each account's usage limit. Click it for the details.

Tip

History, CLAUDE.md, agents and skills stay linked to your main account, so you don't lose anything when you switch accounts.
