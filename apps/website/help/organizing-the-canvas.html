?Tutorial · Canvas

Organize the canvas with groups and notes

Keep work, freelance and personal projects apart, and leave reminders on the canvas.

Beginner4 min read

Help › Canvas

  1. 1Create a group

    Choose Group in the canvas menu (Ctrl G (⌘G on Mac)). Double-click the name to rename it (Work, Freelance, Personal) and right-click to pick a color.

  2. 2Put folders inside

    Drag folders into the group. Blocks snap to the edges or centers of their neighbors.

  3. 3Tidy up in one click

    Right-click the canvas › Organize board, or right-click a group › Organize group. The blocks line up and the group shrinks to fit what's inside.

  4. 4Leave notes

    With the mouse over the canvas, press C to drop a note, like comments in Figma. Delete removes it and undo brings it back.

  5. 5Undo what you don't like

    Ctrl Z (⌘Z on Mac) undoes and Ctrl Shift Z (⇧⌘Z on Mac) redoes any layout change.

Tip

Just ask the command bar: "create a Freelance group with these two folders". It understands both text and voice.
