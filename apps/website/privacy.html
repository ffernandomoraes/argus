Privacy.

What Argus sends off your machine, and what it doesn’t.

Last updated October 31, 2026.

SummaryYour chats and projectsError reportsRequests the app makesThis websiteOpen sourceQuestions

Summary

Argus is an app that runs on your machine. There is no Argus account, there is no Argus server sitting between you and your chats, and the app does not collect the contents of your projects or your chats.

What leaves your machine is your chats with the agents (just as they would from the terminal), a few technical requests and error reports with no personal data. The details are below.

Your chats and projects

Argus runs the Claude Code installed on your machine (and, if you connect them, Codex, Gemini CLI or Cursor CLI), with your own login. What you send in a chat goes to the agent’s provider exactly as it would from the terminal, and is covered by that provider’s privacy policy: Anthropic’s, in Claude’s case.

Projects, chat history, the canvas and settings stay in files on your machine. Argus reads the history that Claude Code itself writes to ~/.claude.

Error reports

In the installed app, errors, crashes and freezes are sent to Sentry so they can be fixed. They are always sent; there is no option to turn them off.

What is sentWhat is not sent
Where the error happened (the function stack and your last actions in the app)Chats and file contents
Argus version, operating system and whether WSL is presentKeys, tokens and email addresses (they are scrubbed)
A random installation identifierYour machine name and your username (they are removed even from folder paths)

Collection of function variables and crash memory snapshots, which could contain bits of chats, is also turned off.

Requests the app makes

What forWhere to
Check for and download updates (on launch and every 5 hours)GitHub
Find out whether there is a new version of Claude Codenpm
Show the usage limit of each accountAnthropic, with your login
Install Claude Code, when you ask for it on the welcome screenclaude.ai (official installer)
Dictation: audio is streamed in real time to Claude’s voice serviceAnthropic, with your login
Local audio transcription (experimental): download the model onceHugging Face

None of these requests carries the contents of your chats, except dictation, which carries the audio you record.

This website

This website is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies and has no analytics or advertising tools.

When you open a page, your browser talks only to GitHub, which hosts the site and provides the repository numbers and download links. GitHub may log your IP address, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The fonts are hosted together with the site.

Open source

Everything on this page can be checked in the Argus source code, which is open on GitHub.

Questions

If you have questions about privacy, reach out through the contact page. If the app starts sending anything new, this page will change too.
